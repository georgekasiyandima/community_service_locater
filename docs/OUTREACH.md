# Outreach — how to take Ubuntu Code into the room

Carry three things, never twelve:

1. The one-pager (printed or PDF)
2. This locator, open on your phone: [https://ubuntu-code-locator.onrender.com](https://ubuntu-code-locator.onrender.com)
3. A time you can sit with them this week

---

## The opening (about 90 seconds)

> I am George from Ubuntu Code. We build simple digital tools for churches, schools, councils and small businesses here in Manicaland. We already shipped the Caritas Mutare website. I want to show you a small tool we are testing in Bvumba Ward 14 and Sherukuru Ward 15 — then hear the problem that is wasting your time.

Then open the demo. Do not explain architecture.

---

## Demo script (45–60 seconds)

1. “A person in Bvumba texts one word.” Tap **CLINIC**. Point out that Sherukuru Clinic is offered as the nearest clinic.
2. Point at Sherukuru Clinic. “She does not walk there for nothing if the nurse is away.”
3. Switch to **Local admin**. “The councillor or parish rep sends this.” Tap **Fix Mukoyi borehole** or type `UPDATE BOREHOLE2 WORKING`.
4. Switch back to Bvumba, tap **BOREHOLE**. “The next person sees the change.”
5. Stop talking. Ask: “Where does this kind of confusion happen for you?”

If they are a school or the Education Department, start with **SCHOOL**, not boreholes.

If they are an SME, do this demo only if they asked about community tools. Otherwise skip to “What we can build for a shop.”

---

## Who you are talking to

### Caritas Mutare / existing relationship

**Ask:** an introduction to one ward or one parish that would try this for a week.  
**Do not ask:** for a diocese-wide rollout.

Follow-up note:

> Thank you for the time. The locator is live as a prototype. If you can name one parish rep or clinic clerk, I will sit with them, replace the sample list with real names, and report back after seven days.

### Fr Kembo / Education Department

**Show:** school open/closed, term notes, a head teacher as admin.  
**Ask:** permission to test with one school, not a policy change.

### Parish priest / parish rep

**Show:** the admin command. Let them type it.  
**Ask:** “Would you be willing to be the number that updates this for your area?”

### Ward councillor / clinic clerk

**Show:** borehole and clinic rows. Be humble about sample data.  
**Ask:** help verifying 10–15 names. Offer to do the typing.

### Head teacher

**Show:** their kind of row. Ask what parents actually phone the office to ask.

### SME / emerging business (Mutare, Rusape, Chipinge, Nyanga, Rusape growth points)

Leave the locator on the table as proof of shipping. Sell from the one-pager:

- “Do people struggle to find you?”
- “Do you take orders on WhatsApp in a messy way?”
- “Do you lose track of stock or bookings?”

**Ask:** a free consult this week. Quote only after you have seen the shop or the books.

### Civic groups, burial societies, savings groups

Listen first. Do not offer a savings ledger in the first meeting. Offer a notice channel or a simple page. Money tools come after trust.

---

## What to send after the meeting

Same day, on WhatsApp:

> It was good to meet. Here is the one-pager, the live demo link, and my number. I can come back for a free consult — no proposal until I have understood the problem.

Attach:

- PDF one-pager
- Demo URL: https://ubuntu-code-locator.onrender.com
- Optional: 60-second video once you have recorded it

Do not attach this architecture folder.

---

## What not to say

- “We will use AI to understand any question.”
- “This already covers the whole district.”
- “I can have the full diocese platform in a month.”
- “BloomTech” as the first credential.
- Any clinic or borehole status presented as official unless a local person confirmed it.

---

## A simple pipeline you can keep in a notebook

| Stage | Meaning | Next action |
|---|---|---|
| Introduced | They have the one-pager | Book a sit-down |
| Demo seen | They used the chat | Ask for one local admin or one shop visit |
| Field list started | Real names collected | Run the test week |
| Case study | Numbers + quote | Ask for a second introduction |
| Paid scope | They want a site or tool | Write a one-page proposal |

Five honest names in this book beat fifty cold messages.

---

## Recording the case-study week

Write these down every evening. They become the second page of the one-pager.

- How many real queries?
- Which word was used most?
- Did an admin update without you prompting?
- What did people ask that the menu could not answer?
- One sentence from the admin, in their words
